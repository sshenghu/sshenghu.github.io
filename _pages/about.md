I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor with the Internet of Things (IoT) Thrust, Information Hub, at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). I received my Ph.D. degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Waterloo in 2025, where I was supervised by [Professor Xuemin (Sherman) Shen](https://uwaterloo.ca/broadband-communications-research-lab/sshen). Before joining HKUST(GZ), I was a postdoctoral fellow at the University of Waterloo, under the supervision of Professor Shen and [Professor Weihua Zhuang](https://uwaterloo.ca/electrical-computer-engineering/profile/wzhuang). I earned my M.A.Sc. and bachelor's degrees from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, where I was supervised by [Professor Ying-Chang Liang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=HybIiJ8AAAAJ&hl=en).

My research focuses on wireless sensing, communications, and computing for AI-empowered mobile agents, devices, and networks. I have published over 30 journal and conference papers in prestigious venues. I was a recipient of the Chinese Government Award for Outstanding Self-Financed Students Abroad, the Best Student Paper Award at IEEE VTC 2025-Fall, and Best Paper Awards at WCSP 2025 and IEEE GLOBECOM 2024. One of my papers was recognized as an [ESI Highly Cited Paper](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8891763/), and my master's thesis was selected for the China Institute of Communications Outstanding Master's Thesis Incentive Program.
