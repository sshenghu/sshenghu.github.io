## Join Our Group

I am recruiting Ph.D. and M.Phil. students for the Spring 2027 intake only. Research Assistant positions are available year-round, with both remote and on-site arrangements possible.

### Research Areas

- Sensing, communications, and control for AI-empowered mobile agents
- Sensing and communications for immersive applications, e.g., mobile augmented reality
- AI-agent-assisted autonomous wireless networks

### What We Look For

- A solid theoretical foundation or research experience related to the research areas above
- Strong interest, self-motivation, and perseverance in conducting high-quality research
- Good English reading, writing, and communication skills, together with a collaborative mindset

### How to Apply

Please email your CV to [shishenghu@hkust-gz.edu.cn](mailto:shishenghu@hkust-gz.edu.cn).

All applicants are automatically considered for scholarships; no separate scholarship application is required.
