## Join Our Team

I am recruiting Ph.D. and M.Phil. students for the Spring and Fall 2027 intakes. Research Assistant positions are available year-round, with both remote and on-site arrangements possible.

### Research Topics

- **Sensing, communications, and control for AI-empowered mobile agents**
- **Sensing, communications, and computing for immersive applications, e.g., mobile augmented reality**
- **AI-agent-assisted autonomous wireless networks**

- A solid theoretical foundation or research experience related to the research topics above
- Strong interest, self-motivation, and perseverance in conducting high-quality research
- Good English reading, writing, and communication skills, together with a collaborative mindset
